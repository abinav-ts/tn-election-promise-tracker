Did the promises get kept, and how fast?

Every promise in the manifestos of the parties that formed the Tamil Nadu government in 2016, 2021 and 2026, checked against official documents: policy notes, budget speeches, Government Orders and audit reports. A promise counts as realised when an official step puts it into operation, with the date of the earliest such step. Nothing here ranks parties or rates governments.

Which judge?

The answers depend on who judged the evidence. Both views are shown; neither is "the truth". The whitepaper compares them against a hand-labelled sample.

Share of promises realised, by month of tenure

Curves use the date each realised promise was first operationalised, as far as the evidence now shows (Kaplan–Meier). The dots are what was documented at each landmark month; the gap between line and dots is evidence that appeared only later.

TVK 2026: a projection, with its assumptions

Projection under historical patterns, built from the two completed terms. It shows what the past would imply, with the assumptions stated below each line; it is not a prediction of what TVK will do. Bands show fitting uncertainty only.

How well do these methods work?

Reverse backtest: the model is fitted on DMK 2021–26 only and asked to predict AIADMK 2016–21 from its early months. Accuracy against a hand-labelled sample and all limitations are in the whitepaper.
