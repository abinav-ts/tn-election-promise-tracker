Promises made. Promises kept?

A neutral, evidence-based look at how Tamil Nadu's ruling parties performed on their promises. Every promise in the manifestos of the parties that formed the government in 2016, 2021 and 2026, checked against official documents: policy notes, budget speeches, Government Orders and audit reports. Nothing here ranks parties or rates governments.

A promise counts as realised when an official step puts it into operation, with the date of the earliest such step.
