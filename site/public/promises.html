Promise explorer

Every promise with its status, date and the evidence behind it. Statuses at earlier months show only what was documented by then. "No evidence found" means the corpus held nothing showing an official step, not that nothing happened.

Think a verdict is wrong? Every promise has a Suggest a correction link. Suggestions are reviewed by hand, are stored separately as GitHub issues, never change a verdict automatically, and are never used by the judging or survival models.
