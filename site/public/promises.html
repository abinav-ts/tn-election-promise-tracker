Why this website exists

Election manifestos are promises made in public. This site lets anyone check, promise by promise, whether an official step followed, when, and on what evidence, with the judge named beside every verdict.

It also lets readers push back: question a verdict, add evidence, or say what is missing. Nothing here ranks parties or rates governments.

How comments are handled. A short word list blocks obvious abuse in your browser. Your comment is then sent as a public GitHub issue. A person reads it, and only approved comments appear below, labelled "visitor comments, unverified". Comments never change a verdict. The word list cannot catch everything, which is why reading by hand is the real filter.
