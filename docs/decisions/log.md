One line per settled decision: ID · date · decision · reason. Settled decisions are not reopened without a new entry.

- **D-002 · 2026-10-05 · No scandal, favourability or composite party scores.** They hide subjective weights, carry defamation risk and invite bias accusations.
- **D-003 · 2026-10-06 · Terms:** DMK 2021–26 (training), AIADMK 2016–21 (backtest target), TVK 2026– (projection). AIADMK 2011–16 only if time allows.
- **D-004 · 2026-10-06 · Terms treated as independent.** A stated assumption in the whitepaper.
- **D-005 · 2026-10-06 · Reverse backtest first** (train DMK-2021 → test AIADMK-2016), for recency. Forward backtest added later; the reverse one is the primary and is never cut.
- **D-006 · 2026-10-06 · Vague promises are decomposed into checkable indicators.**
- **D-007 · 2026-10-06 · The hand-labelled gold set is test-only,** never used for training or prompts.
- **D-008 · 2026-10-06 · Time to realisation is modelled with survival analysis,** right-censored at term end.
- **D-009 · 2026-10-06 · Local corpus + hybrid retrieval** (BM25 + dense), not live web search.
- **D-010 · 2026-10-06 · Every verdict that claims realisation must cite corpus chunks.** Leakage guard.
- **D-011 · 2026-10-06 · Government documents are primary evidence; news is secondary.**
- **D-012 · 2026-10-06 · LLMs:** a free-tier API or Kaggle-hosted open model for bulk steps; Claude in Cowork as the strong judge via file handoff, on a subset (gold set, flagged items, headline promises, TVK).
- **D-013 · 2026-10-06 · Deliverable:** a static website (JSON exported by the pipeline) with a whitepaper page; free static hosting.
- **D-014 · 2026-10-06 · Manifestos may be Tamil.** Tesseract `tam` OCR fallback; promises extracted in English with the verbatim Tamil source kept. Evidence stays English, with Tesseract `eng` as a fallback.
- **D-015 · 2026-10-06 · Solo, 20 days, one conversation per stage, git commit after every stage.**
- **D-016 · 2026-10-07 · Term dates:** AIADMK-2016 sworn in 2016-05-23, DMK-2021 2021-05-07, TVK-2026 2026-05-10; `term_end` = day before the next swearing-in; CM changes within a party's term do not start a new term. Evidence window starts 2016-05-01.
- **D-017 · 2026-10-07 · Tamil evidence is catalogued and paired, not used.** English first; Tamil-only documents (no English counterpart by GO number / fiscal year / date) are candidates, decided later. Amends global §3.
- **D-018 · 2026-10-07 · One document per URL: the latest version** (treated as the post-modification/revocation version). Wayback date fallback uses the first capture of the kept file, not of the URL. Superseded versions are listed in the coverage report.
- **D-019 · 2026-10-07 · `documents` gains `lang`, `date_source`, `discovery`** (global §7). Wayback-capture and undated documents go to a date-review sheet for manual search.
- **D-020 · 2026-10-07 · Third-party trackers are leads only,** never evidence, prompts or labels. Documents found through them are tagged `tracker_lead`; results are checked with and without them.
- **D-021 · 2026-10-07 · Download all English GOs in the window (~7,050, ~9 GB).** Claude builds and tests with `--limit`; the user runs the full download in their own terminal (background jobs don't survive in Claude's environment, and this saves usage).
- **D-022 · 2026-10-07 · Manifestos renamed `{TERM}_{lang}.pdf`;** manifesto parsing belongs to stage 02. Policy notes + Performance Budgets in, Citizen Charters out. 45-min discovery timebox for missing sources, then manual drop.
- **D-023 · 2026-10-08 · Wayback dating also checks the pre-migration URL of the same file** (`cms.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/`), but only captures whose digest equals the kept bytes. Live files matched the earliest old-path capture byte for byte; without this, almost no policy note had a capture. Same date-safety rule as D-018.
- **D-024 · 2026-10-08 · "© Government of Tamil Nadu YYYY" is a year-precision date fallback,** ranked after the Wayback capture, listed for review. Not used if the year is earlier than the fiscal year or the text mentions a later date (leak guards).
- **D-025 · 2026-10-08 · The S1 listing also supplies budget speeches (incl. agriculture), Governor's addresses and state sector policies.** This covers most of the S3 2016–21 and S4 gaps; audit reports, directories and similar files stay excluded.
- **D-026 · 2026-10-08 · `--limit N` = the first N in-scope items per source, in a fixed sha1(URL) order.** It still caps downloads at N and makes a re-run download 0, which is what spec 01 step 3 and acceptance criterion 2 require.
- **D-027 · 2026-10-08 · Human spot-checks cover the 7–10 most problematic items per stage, not 20 random ones.** Saves the user's time and targets failure modes. The pass rule is "every wrong item fixed or logged", not a percentage. Overall accuracy still comes from the stage 06 gold set (global §12.4).
- **D-028 · 2026-10-08 · The anachronism check ignores forward-looking and end-of-period dates** (after "before/by/up to/till/until/with effect from/effective from/w.e.f.", or 31 Dec / 31 Mar / December / March). The limit-20 spot-check found all 10 flags were such dates; flags fell from 21 to 9 on the 80 documents. Review flag only: stored dates are unchanged, and the © year leak guard still uses every date.
- **D-029 · 2026-10-08 · Only definitive failures are `fetch_failed`; temporary ones stay pending.** The first full crawl marked ~2,380 GOs as failed after one connection drop on cms.tn.gov.in, and deferred 1,363 Wayback items after one throttle, because a single failure paused a host for the whole run. Hosts now pause only after 3 consecutive failures, with a doubling cooldown (2 → 30 min). Items wrongly failed by the old rule are retried automatically.
- **D-030 · 2026-10-09 · Downloads have a time limit and PDFs must be complete.** One Wayback capture took 3.5 h, and a CAG file was a truncated 5 MiB capture with no `%%EOF`. Downloads are streamed and aborted after `http.max_download_s` (600 s), as a retryable failure listed in the coverage gaps. A PDF is kept only if it has `%%EOF` at the end and opens in PyMuPDF. A truncated Wayback capture is not a "newer version" under D-018: the latest complete capture is kept, else a fetch failure. Incomplete files already stored are quarantined (moved, not deleted) and fetched again, an exception to raw-file immutability.
- **D-031 · 2026-10-09 · Crawl order: live sources first, slow Wayback-only sources last** (S1 → S2 → S6 → S7 → S3 → S4 → S5), so a slow archive download can't hold up the GOs and scheme pages.
- **D-032 · 2026-10-11 · Listing date vs. the document's own issue date: the later one wins.** In the full run ~6 GOs were dated 9–13 days after their listing date; using the listing date would make them visible early in cutoff runs. Applies only to a day-precise text date; the usual case (GO dated before its listing) is unchanged. Full corpus: 49 GOs use the later date, 7 of them probably because OCR misread the year (later, never earlier, so no leak).
- **D-033 · 2026-10-11 · A live PDF without `%%EOF` is accepted if its size matches the server's Content-Length and it opens with ≥ 1 page.** One case: the Industries policy note 2023-24, whose server copy has no `%%EOF` but 241 readable pages. Not applied to Wayback captures (a truncated capture reports its own truncated length).
- **D-034 · 2026-10-11 · Build-time clarifications from the stage 01 spec-vs-build check** (flagged to the user, revisable): (a) Tamil pairing includes the speech subtype in the FY key, so interim and revised speeches of one year aren't cross-paired; (b) an unresolved GO lead triggers a re-read of that year's GO listings for all departments; the Wayback step of spec 01 §4.7 is dropped because a GO's file name can't be derived from its number. No tracker found cites GO numbers, so this path was not exercised on real data.

- **D-035 · 2026-10-11 · Legacy-font Tamil manifestos are converted by font mapping, not OCR** (amends D-014 and global §3.1). AIADMK-2016 (and the unused DMK-2021 Tamil file) are typed in VANAVIL-family legacy fonts whose glyphs map deterministically to Unicode. On the real file the vendored Open-Tamil VANAVIL table gave 99.96 % Tamil characters, and OCR would have to guess at typed text. Conversion is per span, by font name; fake-bold overprints (each line printed 4×) are removed. Tamil-share and orthographic-validity checks gate every page; a failing page falls back to OCR, then vision. User's decision.
- **D-036 · 2026-10-11 · TVK-2026 (image-only, 150 ppi) is OCR'd with Tesseract `tam` behind a calibrated quality gate; failing pages are transcribed by Claude in a Cowork file handoff** (global §6.4). Tesseract's error rate at 150 ppi is calibrated on AIADMK pages rendered as images and compared with their font-mapped text. Transcriptions are cross-checked against OCR and committed. User's decision.
- **D-037 · 2026-10-11 · Bulk backend = Gemini API free tier** (resolves Q-03). It plugs straight into `ept.llm`, handles Tamil, and the user has a key. The exact Flash-family model is chosen by the spec 02 backend test. The key lives in `GEMINI_API_KEY` in `.env` and is never committed, printed or logged. Kaggle is the fallback. This replaces the head-to-head Gemini-vs-Kaggle test in global §6.1, because a Kaggle model would need a file handoff.
- **D-038 · 2026-10-11 · Promise rules (resolves Q-04).** (a) Advocacy commitments ("urge/press the Union government") are promises; past achievements, criticism and value statements are not. (b) Split only into independently deliverable parts; means stay with their goal; a list under one action is one promise. (c) Within-term duplicates: the later one is dropped and logged in `promise_dedup.csv` (no schema field). (d) `parent_group` = the manifesto unit (item) a promise came from (global §7 wording updated). User approved.
- **D-039 · 2026-10-11 · Promise IDs are frozen per term after stage 02 sign-off.** The signed-off rows are committed to `data/frozen/promises_{term}.jsonl` and loaded from there; re-extraction is refused. Gold labels, retrievals and verdicts key on promise IDs, and the LLM cache is not committed. Changing a frozen term needs a new decision entry. User approved.
- **D-040 · 2026-10-11 · One generic manifesto reader and cutter, driven by per-file layout profiles** (`config/manifestos.yaml`). The three manifestos differ in file type (text layer, legacy font, scan) and item markers (running numbers, bullets, per-section numbers), so per-file configuration is unavoidable. It describes typography only; the extraction prompt, checks and thresholds are the same for every party (global §9.2).
